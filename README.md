# Spotify-Data-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI-
A Power BI dashboard exploring Spotify’s top songs, artists, and music trends.
## 📊 Project Overview  
This project provides a **data-driven analysis of Spotify music trends** using an interactive **Power BI dashboard**.  
It explores how different artists, albums, and songs perform across time, focusing on factors such as **popularity**, **release patterns**, **explicit content**, and **average song duration**.  
The goal is to gain insights into listener preferences and understand what makes a song successful on Spotify.

---

## 🧠 Objectives
- Analyze Spotify’s top songs and artists performance.  
- Compare **popularity trends** across months and years.  
- Understand the difference between **album types (single, album, compilation)**.  
- Study **explicit vs. non-explicit** content distribution.  
- Visualize **average duration and song popularity** metrics.

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Taylor Swift**, **Billie Eilish**, and **Bad Bunny** dominate the top charts.  
- The **average popularity** of tracks is around **89.6**, showing strong audience engagement.  
- **Singles** form the majority (562 songs), followed by **albums** (269).  
- **Explicit songs (17K)** outnumber **non-explicit (11K)**, showing a trend toward expressive lyrics.  
- Highest number of **distinct songs released in mid-year (June–August)**.  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – for data modeling and visualization  
- **Excel / CSV Dataset** – for data preprocessing  
- **Spotify Dataset** – containing song metadata, artist info, and popularity scores  

---

## 📂 Dashboard Features
- 🎤 **Songs by Artist** – Top-performing artists by number of songs  
- 🕒 **Songs by Year & Month** – Trend of releases and distinct songs count  
- 💿 **Songs by Album Type** – Split by singles, albums, and compilations  
- 🔥 **Average Popularity by Month** – Identifies high-engagement periods  
- 🚫 **Explicit vs. Non-Explicit** – Breakdown of lyrical content trends  
- ⏱️ **Average Song Duration** – Duration analysis across albums and years
